01 — About

Security is architecture,
not a checklist.

I help teams move from perimeter thinking to zero-trust design — mapping trust boundaries before writing a single control.

Over the past eight years I've helped fintech, SaaS, and infrastructure teams design systems that survive failure and resist attack. My work sits at the intersection of threat modeling, identity architecture, and cloud-native security.

I believe the best security control is the one that makes the insecure path the harder path — so I design for developer ergonomics as much as for defense in depth.
